# Equipment and Account Requests
Document ID: 05_Equipment_and_Account_Requests
Version: 1.0

## Standard setup
Depending on the role, employees may receive a laptop, charger, headset, and approved software. Managers approve role-specific hardware and access according to business need.

## Requesting equipment
Open an IT service desk ticket, choose Hardware Request, identify the device or accessory, provide business justification, and obtain required manager approval. Do not include passwords or confidential customer data.

## Requesting system access
Choose Access Request, specify the application, required permission level, and business reason. The application owner or manager approves access before IT provisions it.

## First login and MFA
Use the activation instructions sent through an approved channel, create a strong unique password, and enroll in multifactor authentication. Never share one-time codes with anyone.

## Troubleshooting and offboarding
For broken devices or locked accounts, submit an incident ticket through the service desk. Report lost devices immediately. On departure, return assigned equipment and follow account deactivation instructions.
