# HR RAG sample corpus

Seven fictional HR documents, each provided as .docx for editing and .txt for direct ingestion. No real employee records are included.

Use the .txt files for chunking and retrieval. Preserve the document title, Document ID, and section heading as metadata. Suggested chunk size: one section per chunk. Do not treat sample deadlines or procedures as official policy.

Example queries:
- What should I complete on my first day?
- Where do I submit employment forms?
- How do I enroll in benefits?
- Where can I see required training?
- How do I request software access?
- How do I report harassment?
- Who handles payroll questions?
