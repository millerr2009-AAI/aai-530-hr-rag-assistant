# HR Policies
Document ID: 06_HR_Policies
Version: 1.0

## Code of conduct
Employees are expected to act professionally, treat coworkers respectfully, protect company information, and report suspected misconduct through designated channels.

## Equal opportunity and respectful workplace
The organization aims to provide a workplace free from unlawful discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. Report concerns to HR or an approved reporting channel; managers should escalate complaints promptly.

## Attendance and timekeeping
Employees follow their assigned schedule, record time accurately where required, and notify managers about absences using approved procedures. Applicable laws and individual employment terms take precedence over this sample.

## Leave and accommodations
Time off, protected leave, and reasonable accommodations are handled under applicable law and official company policies. Contact HR for eligibility and confidential case handling; do not assume universal accrual or approval rules.

## Data protection and policy precedence
Use approved systems, follow least-privilege access, and report security incidents promptly. This document is illustrative only: the signed employee handbook, current policy notices, plan documents, and applicable law govern if there is a conflict.
