# Benefits Enrollment
Document ID: 03_Benefits_Enrollment
Version: 1.0

## Overview
Eligible employees may be offered medical, dental, vision, retirement, life insurance, or other benefits. Actual eligibility, costs, waiting periods, and plan terms are determined by the employer’s official plan documents.

## How to enroll
Open the secure benefits portal, review plan summaries and dependent eligibility, compare coverage and payroll deductions, select plans, add eligible dependents, and save the confirmation receipt.

## Enrollment window
Use the exact enrollment deadline shown in the benefits portal or welcome materials. This sample knowledge base intentionally does not specify a universal deadline. Missing a window may limit changes until the next permitted enrollment period or qualifying event.

## Qualifying life events
Certain events such as marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage may allow a midyear change under plan rules. Notify benefits HR promptly and consult the official plan documents for evidence and deadlines.

## Help and privacy
Contact the benefits team for eligibility or coverage questions. Never place medical information, Social Security numbers, or dependent identity records in a general IT ticket or RAG chat.
